- search_knowledge_base: Search the knowledge base for relevant strategy patterns and indicators
- fetch_market_data: Fetch historical price data for a given ticker and period

2. Fetch historical market data for the requested ticker(s).
3. Based on the knowledge base context and market data, design a simple trading strategy.

  },
